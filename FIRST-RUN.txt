BOOK CAPTURE AI v0.4 - FIRST RUN

1. Open Kindle for Web / an ebook reader / browser on Windows.
2. Run BookCaptureAI.exe.
3. Click "ウィンドウ更新" and choose the ebook window.
4. Click "画面範囲を選択" and drag around the whole visible book area.
5. Choose the page-turn key (Kindle is often ← or → depending on layout).
6. For a two-page spread, keep "見開きを中央から2ページに自動分割する" ON.
7. Japanese books: use "右 → 左". Western/left-to-right books: use "左 → 右".
8. Click "キャプチャ開始".
9. Original spread screenshots stay in images/.
10. Split single-page images are saved in images-split/.
11. PDF / searchable PDF / OCR TXT are generated from the split pages when split mode is ON.

OCR:
- Tesseract OCR + Japanese/English language data are bundled in the Windows package.
- No separate OCR installation is required.
- OCR runs locally on this PC.

Notes:
- Split mode currently divides at the exact center of the selected capture area.
- For a normal one-page Kindle view, turn split mode OFF.
- If a cover or chapter page occupies only one side, an extra blank/partial half may be created; the original spread is always preserved.

Safety / rights:
- This app does not remove DRM or bypass screenshot protection.
- Use only for content and purposes you are authorized to copy.
