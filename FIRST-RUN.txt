BOOK CAPTURE AI - FIRST RUN

1. Open Kindle / an ebook reader / browser on Windows.
2. Run BookCaptureAI.exe.
3. Click "ウィンドウ更新" and choose the ebook window.
4. Click "画面範囲を選択" and drag around only the book page.
5. Choose the page-turn key (Kindle is often ← or → depending on layout).
6. Click "キャプチャ開始".
7. The app stops automatically when the same screen repeats, or at the max page count.
8. A PDF is created in the chosen output folder.

OCR:
- Tesseract OCR + Japanese/English language data are bundled in the Windows package.
- No separate OCR installation is required.
- OCR runs locally on this PC.

Safety / rights:
- This app does not remove DRM or bypass screenshot protection.
- Use only for content and purposes you are authorized to copy.
